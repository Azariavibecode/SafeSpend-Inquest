## POLICY SAFE-SPEND-001

This sponsor-declared demonstration policy permits executed Safe transactions only when the recipient is explicitly named in the policy, the value is within the stated raw-unit ceiling, and the calldata represents an ordinary payment for security auditing. Delegate calls, token approvals, ownership changes, and transfers described as marketing, entertainment, or personal expenses are forbidden.

This document declares a test policy. It does not prove ownership of a Safe, execution of a transaction, or the official policy of any DAO.
